This cpu has 16 registers. 7 specialised, 8 general purpose and 1 blank for when non applicable.

Specialised:
- pc (Program Counter)
- acc (Accumulator)
- ir (Instruction Register)
- flz (Zero flag)
- fls (Sign flag (pos/neg))
- flc (Carry flag)
- flo (Overflow Flag)

General:
- ra
- rb
- rc
- rd
- re
- rf
- rg
- rh

0x0 - No Register
0x1 - ra
0x2 - rb
0x3 - rc
0x4 - rd
0x5 - re
0x6 - rf
0x7 - rg
0x8 - rh
0x9 - pc
0xA - acc
0xB - ir
0xC - flz
0xD - fls
0xE - flc
0xF - flo
